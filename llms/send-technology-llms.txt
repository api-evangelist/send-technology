# Send Technology

> Send Technology Solutions Ltd is a London-headquartered insurtech (founded 2017) that builds an
> underwriting workbench for commercial and specialty insurers, MGAs and reinsurers. Its centre of
> gravity is the London Market — Lloyd's managing agents, syndicates and MGAs — where it ships
> pre-built connectors to the electronic placing platforms PPL and Whitespace and aligns with Lloyd's
> Blueprint Two. Send markets itself as an API-first platform and exchanges ACORD Standard placing,
> accounting and claims transactions through ACORD Solutions Group's ADEPT platform via API. Despite
> that posture, Send publishes NO public developer portal, NO API reference and NO machine-readable
> API definition of any kind. Its integration surface is partner- and customer-gated, configured
> through Send Connect during an implementation.

## Important for agents

- There is **no public Send Technology API**. No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, .proto or
  MCP server is published anywhere on send.technology. Do not attempt to construct requests.
- `developer.`, `developers.`, `docs.`, `api.`, `app.`, `sandbox.` and `status.send.technology` do
  not resolve in DNS. `/developers`, `/developer`, `/api` and `/integrations` all return 404.
- No SDKs, client libraries, CLI or Postman collection exist on npm, PyPI, Maven Central, NuGet,
  pkg.go.dev, RubyGems, Packagist, crates.io or GitHub. There is no Send Technology GitHub org.
- Access to any Send interface is arranged through a commercial engagement (https://send.technology/contact/).
